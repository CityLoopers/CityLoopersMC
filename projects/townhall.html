Town Hall Upgrade Project

This page contains information about the Town Hall Upgrade Project.

Initiating in early 2024, The Town Hall Upgrade Project is a significant development in the city of Aurora, focusing on the Town Hall interchange station. This pivotal project involves a comprehensive upgrade, including a total reconstruction of the North South station and the establishment and completion of the East West Line at Town Hall.

Following an extensive rebuild and the excavation of new, deeper tunnels, the North South Line has now resumed operations at the Town Hall station. This marks a milestone in the city's infrastructure enhancement, promising improved connectivity and convenience for its residents.

The newly built station boasts advanced facilities and improved accessibility, ensuring a seamless interchange between the North South and East West Lines. The completion of the East West Line is anticipated to further streamline citywide transportation and foster economic growth by connecting key districts within Aurora.

The North South Line at Town Hall station now boasts a range of state-of-the-art amenities following its reopening, including high-speed escalators, interactive information kiosks, an automated ticketing system, and robust security measures. These enhancements have significantly elevated the station's functionality and safety standards. The East West Line is expected to be fully operational by late 2025, which will further enhance Aurora's transportation infrastructure and stimulate economic growth by linking critical urban areas.
