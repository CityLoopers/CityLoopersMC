Ready to make your mark on the City Loopers Minecraft server? Apply for the Builder rank today!

Application Requirements

To apply for the Builder rank, you must meet the following criteria:

  • Meaningful contributions to the server through new structures or improvements.
  • OR at least one year of active participation on the server.
  • A positive and respectful attitude towards all players.
  • A commitment to following our server's rules and regulations.

Please note: The application process is subjective, and we reserve the right to decline any application.
