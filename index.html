Server Information

Builder Ranks

The following are the builder ranks and their requirements.

Guest

Anyone can join the server and explore the world, but cannot build or modify anything. To apply for a builder rank, guests must fill out a form on the server website and provide some examples of their previous builds.

Builder

Builders can build anywhere in the world, as long as they respect the server rules and guidelines. They can also use basic commands such as /warp, /home, /tpa, etc.

To advance to the next rank, builders must build at least 10 structures and receive positive feedback from the staff and other players.

Architect

Architects can build anywhere in the world, as long as they respect the existing theme and style. They can also use advanced commands such as /worldedit, /voxelsniper, /copy, /paste, etc.

To advance to the next rank, architects must build at least 20 structures and create some impressive landmarks or monuments that showcase their skills and creativity.

Engineer

Engineers are responsible for designing and maintaining the transport infrastructure of the world, such as roads, bridges, railways, subways, airports, etc. They can use all the commands available to architects, as well as some special ones such as /train, /cart, /sign, etc.

To advance to the next rank, engineers must build at least 30 structures and connect them with various modes of transport.

Mayor

Mayors are the leaders and managers of the world. They can build anywhere and use any commands they want. They can also approve or reject new builds, promote other players to builder, etc.

To become a mayor, a player must be nominated by another mayor and approved by a majority vote of all the mayors.

Build Restrictions

Definition of Towns

Towns are defined as any collection of 3 or more buildings built by a single builder

Minimum distance between towns

Without express permission of another town's government, a town may not build within 300 blocks of another town and is discouraged from building within 500 blocks of another town.

Formation of Local Government Areas

Local Government Areas may be formed to allow multiple towns to build in close proximity to each other and provide their own public transportation services. If forming an LGA, please alert the State Government.

This is an option for towns that want to cooperate and collaborate with each other on a larger scale.

A Local Government Area (LGA) is a group of towns that agree to form a regional alliance and share some common goals and interests

An LGA can have its own name, flag, council, policies, projects and services

Transportation connections to Aurora

Aurora is the main and largest city of the server. It is expected that your town has either a road or rail connection to Aurora.

Claiming inactive towns

Inactive Towns (where the owner hasn’t logged in for more than 3 months) may be claimed by another user by submitting a ticket on the discord server.

State government authority

The State Government (Staff Team) has ultimate jurisdiction over all towns and builds within towns and can override planning decisions

Town proposal submission

It is expected (but not required) that members submit proposals for a town before building
